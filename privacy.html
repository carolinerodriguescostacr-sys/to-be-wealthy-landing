Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Wealth By Lin is the public brand for TO BE WEALTHY. The seller of each product is identified on its Hotmart checkout and purchase receipt. For questions about this policy or your personal information, contact wealthbylin@gmail.com.

Information we handle

  • Account and access: email address, account identifier, sign-in requests, session information, and product access rights. The platform uses an email code; it does not ask you to create a password.
  • Purchases: products purchased, transaction identifier, payment status, purchase and refund dates, and buyer email supplied by Hotmart. Hotmart handles checkout and payment information under its own policies. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Platform activity: optional profile details, journey progress, exercise responses, and records you choose to enter in the Guided Journey.
  • Community and support: submissions you choose to make, moderation decisions, and messages sent to our support email.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device information, request times, and security logs processed by our hosting providers.

How we use information

We use it to create and protect accounts, send sign-in codes, confirm purchases, deliver purchased products, maintain progress, moderate submissions, answer support requests, prevent abuse, and meet applicable accounting or legal obligations. We do not use private exercise answers to make lending, insurance, employment, or other eligibility decisions.

Providers and international processing

Hotmart processes purchases; Supabase provides account and database services; Render hosts the app; email providers deliver authentication and transactional messages; and GitHub Pages hosts the public website. These providers may process information in countries other than yours. We do not sell personal information. The public website uses Google Analytics 4 only after a visitor accepts optional analytics. It measures page views and clicks on the three checkout buttons; these clicks do not confirm a purchase. We do not send names, email addresses, exercise answers, or checkout payment information to Analytics.

Cookies

The app uses an essential secure session cookie to keep you signed in. The public website does not load Google Analytics or set its optional analytics cookies until you select Accept analytics. If accepted, Google Analytics may set cookies such as _ga to help count visits and checkout-button clicks. Select Decline to keep it off. You can revisit Analytics preferences in the website footer to change your choice; declining later stops future tracking and removes accessible analytics cookies. We do not use advertising analytics or personalized ads.

Retention and security

We keep account, purchase, access, and user-created records while needed to provide the service, handle refunds or disputes, and satisfy applicable obligations. We review deletion requests against those obligations. We use encrypted connections, access controls, and restricted server credentials, though no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us to confirm what information we hold, provide access, correct inaccurate information, delete eligible information, or explain how it is used and shared. Additional rights may apply where you live. Email wealthbylin@gmail.com; we may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder. You can stop using the service and sign out at any time. For purchase refunds, use the Hotmart process shown at checkout.

Children and changes

The products are intended for adults. Please do not provide personal information for a child through the app or community. We may update this policy as features and providers change; the current version and date will be posted here.
